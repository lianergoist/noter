<p>DavNotes is a simple note app for Android. Your notes are stored as VJOURNAL entries in a CalDAV collection on your own server and synced both ways.</p>
<p>It works offline. Changes are saved on the phone right away and sent to the server as soon as you are online again.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Create, edit, search and delete notes with a title, text and tags.</li>
<li>Light formatting written as Markdown: headings, bold, italic, lists and indentation, with a toolbar above the keyboard. Notes stay plain text that other apps can read.</li>
<li>Links in the text can be opened in the browser.</li>
<li>Open text and Markdown files as new notes, also via "Open with" and "Share", and save a note as a file.</li>
<li>Saves automatically while you type and when you leave a note.</li>
<li>Syncs when you open the app, after every change, with pull to refresh and about every 15 minutes.</li>
<li>Never overwrites a note blindly. If a note was changed both here and somewhere else, your version is saved as a new note with "(conflict)" in the title. Text is never merged.</li>
<li>Shares the collection with other clients, such as jtx Board via DAVx⁵. Properties set by other apps are kept unchanged when you edit a note.</li>
<li>Finds your collections on the server and can create a new notes collection.</li>
<li>The password is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore. HTTPS only.</li>
<li>In English, Danish, German, French and Spanish.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Requirements</b></p>
<p>A CalDAV server that supports sync-collection (RFC 6578). DavNotes is developed and tested with Radicale 3.</p>
<p>No ads, no tracking, no accounts other than your own server.</p>
